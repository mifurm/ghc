# 20 Ways to Use GitHub Copilot as a Product Manager Across the SDLC

1. **Requirements → Issues** — Describe a feature in natural language; have Copilot draft structured GitHub Issues with acceptance criteria and sub-tasks.

2. **Competitive Research** — Use `/research` to analyze competitor repos, APIs, or documentation and summarize findings.

3. **PRD Generation** — Prompt Copilot to turn rough notes into a formatted PRD with user stories, edge cases, and success metrics.

4. **Sprint Planning** — Feed a backlog and ask Copilot to estimate complexity, identify dependencies, and suggest sprint groupings.

5. **Architecture Review** — Point Copilot at your codebase and ask "how does feature X work?" to quickly understand technical constraints before scoping.

6. **Prototype & POC** — Describe a UI or workflow; let Copilot scaffold a working prototype to validate ideas with stakeholders before committing engineering effort.

7. **PR Review for PMs** — Use `/review` or ask Copilot to summarize a PR's user-facing impact in plain language, so you can review without reading code.

8. **Release Notes & Changelogs** — After a milestone, ask Copilot to scan merged PRs and generate customer-facing release notes.

9. **Metrics & Analytics** — Ask Copilot to write SQL queries or dashboards against your analytics DB to answer product questions (funnel drop-off, feature adoption, etc.).

10. **Post-Mortem Analysis** — Feed incident timelines or bug reports and have Copilot draft a structured post-mortem with root cause, impact, and action items.

11. **Customer Feedback Synthesis** — Paste support tickets, survey responses, or NPS comments and have Copilot categorize themes, sentiment, and top feature requests.

12. **API Documentation Review** — Ask Copilot to review your public API docs for completeness, consistency, and developer experience gaps before launch.

13. **A/B Test Design** — Describe a hypothesis; have Copilot draft the experiment plan including variants, metrics, sample size, and statistical significance criteria.

14. **Dependency & Risk Mapping** — Point Copilot at your repo and ask it to identify third-party dependencies, license risks, or outdated packages that could affect your roadmap.

15. **Stakeholder Updates** — Feed recent PRs, issues, and milestones; have Copilot generate a weekly status email or slide deck summary for leadership.

16. **User Journey Mapping** — Describe your product flows and ask Copilot to generate Mermaid diagrams visualizing user journeys, identifying friction points.

17. **Compliance & Accessibility Audit** — Ask Copilot to scan your frontend for WCAG violations, GDPR data handling patterns, or missing privacy controls.

18. **Technical Debt Prioritization** — Have Copilot analyze code quality signals (complexity, duplication, test coverage gaps) and recommend what to pay down and when.

19. **Onboarding Documentation** — Ask Copilot to generate a "new team member" guide by scanning your repo structure, README files, and CI/CD pipelines to explain how the project works.

20. **Feature Flag Management** — Have Copilot audit your codebase for stale feature flags, generate a cleanup plan, and draft the rollout/rollback strategy for upcoming launches.
